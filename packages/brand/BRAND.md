# Clue AI brand

- **Mark:** rounded-square play button; the triangle is cut by a magnifier loop; lime spark dot on the lens (the "clue").
- **Wordmark:** "clue" lowercase, Space Grotesk Bold, lime dot over the "u"; full name "Clue AI".
- **Colors:** Ink `#0E0B1F`, Paper `#FAF8F3`, Violet `#6C4DFF`, Lime `#C8FF3D`, Coral `#FF5C7A`.
- **Usage:** keep clear space equal to the mark's corner radius; minimum size 16px for the mark; never recolor the lime dot; lime text on Paper fails contrast, use it only on Ink/Violet.
- **Files:** `clue-mark.svg`, `clue-lockup.svg`. PNG icons and OG images are generated in Phase 2.
